Questions people ask before they buy

Who are these reports best for?

Best fit (B2C): local service businesses (plumbers, salons, daycares, clinics, gyms, trades), e-commerce / DTC brands, online service providers selling to consumers, creators and personal brands with a public following, and small SaaS selling to small businesses.

Also works (B2B service-style): agencies, consultancies, professional services (law/accounting), and B2B SaaS — provided you have a meaningful public presence (website, content, LinkedIn, reviews, ads).

Not suitable: B2B industrial / wholesale / trade-only businesses (where sales happen via reps, RFPs and trade shows, not public channels), enterprise-sales-only businesses, and adult content / OnlyFans-style creators (public data on those platforms is too restricted for the quality of report we deliver). If that's you, please don't order — message us at service@barebaysidelabs.com first.

Is $747 really worth it for a one-off report?

If you spend $1,000/mo or more on marketing, the report almost certainly pays for itself the first time it catches a winning ad angle you can copy, a service gap you can fill, or a wasted channel you can cancel. Most customers recoup the $747 in the first month of acting on it.

What if I don't know who my 5 competitors are?

Fill the brief with your category + suburbs and we'll pick the 5 most relevant based on who's actually competing for your customers. You approve the list before we start. If you want to swap any, no problem.

Can't I just do this myself with online tools?

You can — buy the subscriptions, learn them, and run the audits yourself. Most owners don't have the weeks that takes, and the tools hand you raw metrics, not decisions. We do the digging across all 8 channels for you and turn it into meaning: a ranked plan, not a dashboard you're left to interpret. We tell you why a rival is winning, what you can copy, and exactly what to do about it — once, for a flat $747, with nothing to learn and no subscription to cancel.

What if my competitors don't run ads?

The report still works — we score 8 channels. If they're weak on ads, the report calls it out as an opportunity you can exploit. (Plus: "nobody in this category runs ads" is often a lie. When you look properly, there's usually at least one rival running something you hadn't seen.)

Is 3 days really enough to go deep?

Yes, because we've built the pipelines to do the heavy collection in the background while analysts focus on interpretation.

Can I keep this updated after?

A private online report — a link only you have, with 11 tabbed sections and everything linked back to the source evidence. There's no login and no password to lose, and nothing to install: open it on a phone or a desktop, and forward the link to anyone on your team. It stays live, and it's yours to keep.

I'm running a small business. Is this overkill?

Only if "overkill" means knowing exactly what the five people eating your lunch are doing, while your competitors guess. Scale doesn't matter — clarity does. If you spend any real money on marketing, you need this.
